Peace of Mind Across Borders

We Treat Your Parents Like Our Own Family

Living thousands of miles away in the Gulf, Europe, or America, sending your elderly parents on pilgrimage can feel daunting. Will they navigate airport terminals safely? Will the hotel be too far from the Haram? Who will push their wheelchair? At NOOR-E-HARAM, our senior tour directors personally oversee your parents from their departure airport in India to the sacred courtyards of Makkah and Madinah.

🛫

1. Airport Terminal Escort

Parents are received at Mumbai, Bangalore, or Hubli airport by our staff. We handle baggage drop, immigration clearance, wheelchair assistance, and seat boarding.

♿

2. Dedicated Wheelchair Khadim

No exhaustion or physical strain. A trained, compassionate Arabic/Urdu-speaking assistant pushes your parents through all 7 circuits of Tawaf and Sa'i with reverence.

🏨

3. Zero-Incline Hotels Next to Haram

We strictly eliminate hill walking or shuttle buses. Accommodation is situated within 50 to 250 meters of the marble courtyard on a completely flat, wheelchair-accessible path.

Hygienic 3-course buffet catering with traditional South and North Indian flavors (mild spices suitable for seniors), fresh seasonal fruits, and continuous hot chai/water.
