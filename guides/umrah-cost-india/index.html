Umrah Cost from India (2026–2027)

A realistic, transparent breakdown of real pilgrimage expenses: return flights, Saudi electronic visas, hotel proximities, dining, and hidden fee prevention.

Pilgrims planning their sacred journey from India frequently face confusing quotations with hidden exclusions. At NOOR-E-HARAM, we believe in complete financial transparency. Below is an honest breakdown of the components that make up the total cost of a 15-day Umrah journey.

1. The 5 Core Expense Components

Component Average Cost Share What Determines the Price
Return Airfare 40% – 50% Direct flights (Mumbai/Delhi/Bangalore to JED/MED) vs connecting flights; early booking vs last-minute peak dates.
Hotel Accommodation 30% – 35% Distance to Haram courtyard (walking vs shuttle bus); room sharing category (Double, Triple, Quad or Quint).
Visa & Medical Insurance 10% – 15% Official Saudi Ministry fee, platform processing, and mandatory CHI emergency medical insurance.
Food & Catering 8% – 10% 3 daily Indian buffet meals prepared by Indian chefs in Makkah and Madinah vs eating at local restaurants.
Ground Transportation & Ziyarat 5% – 7% Luxury air-conditioned buses for airport transfers, intercity travel (Makkah-Madinah), and historical tours.

3. Hidden Costs to Watch Out For with Unverified Agents

Financial Safety Advisory for Pilgrims

When comparing quotations from various operators, always verify if: (1) Mandatory Saudi medical insurance is included in the visa fee, (2) Displayed hotel distances are actual walking meters or require separate taxi/shuttle expenses, (3) 3 meals a day are guaranteed or excluded, and (4) Return airport transfers in Saudi Arabia are fully covered.

Is personal shopping money included in the package price?

No. Personal spending money for dates (Ajwa), gifts, extra Zamzam, and personal shopping is not included. We advise carrying approximately 500 to 1,500 Saudi Riyals (SAR) per person in cash or international debit cards for personal expenses.
