Nusuk App Rawdah Permit Guide 2026–2027

Complete walkthrough for securing your prayer permit inside Riyadh ul-Jannah (The Noble Rawdah) at Masjid an-Nabawi. Avoid common booking pitfalls, understand official Ministry slot rules, and review dedicated visiting windows for men and women.

The Spiritual Importance of Riyadh ul-Jannah

The Prophet Muhammad (ﷺ) said: "Between my house and my pulpit is a garden from the gardens of Paradise" (Sahih al-Bukhari). For every pilgrim traveling to the illuminated city of Al-Madinah Al-Munawwarah, offering two rak'ahs of prayer and sending Salam upon the Messenger of Allah inside the green carpeted area known as Al-Rawdah Al-Sharifah is a life-defining spiritual milestone.

To preserve peace, prevent overcrowding, and guarantee dignified access for every pilgrim, the Saudi Ministry of Hajj and Umrah mandates that every single individual must hold a confirmed digital permit issued via the official Nusuk application. Paper passes, unofficial agency tokens, or unauthorized gate requests are strictly rejected at security checkpoints.

Crucial Nusuk Rules Every Indian Pilgrim Must Know

  • The 365-Day Restriction: You can only book a Rawdah permit once every 365 days per passport/visa. Ensure you select your date and time with absolute precision; cancellations may not immediately reopen your quota.
  • Individual Bookings Required: There are no group or family permits for the Rawdah. Every adult and child aged 5 or older must have their own registered account or be added as an authenticated companion with a unique visa number.
  • 24–48 Hour Database Sync: After your Umrah visa is issued by the Saudi Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MOFA), allow 24 to 48 hours for the Saudi data bridge to sync before attempting to register your Nusuk visitor account.
  • Strict Punctuality: Arrive at your designated courtyard holding area at least 30 to 45 minutes prior to your time window. Security scans the dynamic QR code at the gate; expired slots will result in denied entry.

Official Visiting Hours: Men vs. Women

The General Authority for the Care of the Two Holy Mosques establishes segregated visiting hours to allow pilgrims to pray in complete serenity:

Category Daily Operating Windows Designated Assembly Gate
Men (Morning) 2:00 AM – Fajr Adhan Southern Courtyard — Gate 37 (Bab Bilal area)
Men (Afternoon/Night) 11:20 AM – Isha Adhan Southern Courtyard — Gate 37
Women (Morning) 6:00 AM (Post-Fajr) – 11:00 AM Northern Courtyard — Gate 24, 29, or 32
Women (Night) 9:30 PM (Post-Isha) – 2:00 AM Northern Courtyard — Gate 24, 29, or 32

Note: Visiting slots are subject to minor adjustments by mosque administration during Ramadan, Hajj season, and Friday congregation times. Always rely on the exact time stamped on your active Nusuk permit.

Step-by-Step: How to Book on the Nusuk App

Step 1

Install & Select Visitor

Download "Nusuk" from Google Play Store or Apple App Store. Select English, tap "New User", and choose "Visitor" (not Citizen/Resident).

Step 2

Enter Visa Credentials

Enter your Passport Number, Visa Number (printed on your Saudi E-Visa), Nationality (India), Date of Birth, Email, and Create Password. Complete OTP verification.

Step 3

Select Rawdah Service

Navigate to "Prophet's Mosque Services". Select "Praying in the Noble Rawdah - Men" or "Praying in the Noble Rawdah - Women" carefully. Choosing the wrong gender cannot be corrected at the gate.

Step 4

Choose Green Slot & Save QR

Select your preferred date. Available time slots appear in green (low crowd) or yellow (moderate). Confirm your slot, take a screenshot of the QR code immediately, and keep a printed backup in case mobile networks drop near the mosque.

💡

Expert Tip: Internet Dead Zones & QR Precautions

Masjid an-Nabawi's outer courtyards accommodate over 500,000 worshippers during peak hours. Local Saudi telecom signals (STC, Mobily, Zain) experience severe packet loss inside the perimeter. Always capture a high-resolution screenshot of your active Nusuk permit before leaving your hotel room. Security guards will scan the QR code directly from your gallery.

Etiquette and Sunnah Inside the Rawdah

Once inside the sacred boundaries of the Rawdah, marked by the distinctive green carpets (unlike the crimson carpets of the rest of the mosque):

  • Perform Two Rak'ahs of Tahiyyat al-Masjid: Pray calmly without rushing or pushing fellow worshippers.
  • Supplicate with Humility: Ask Allah for forgiveness, health, blessings for your family, and righteous deeds. Supplications in the Rawdah are widely accepted.
  • Send Salam to the Prophet (ﷺ): For men walking through the pathway towards Bab as-Salam, greet the Messenger of Allah (ﷺ), followed by Abu Bakr as-Siddiq (RA) and Umar ibn al-Khattab (RA) with lowered voices and deep reverence.
  • Respect Time Allocations: Mosque staff typically allow 10 to 15 minutes inside the enclosure before guiding groups forward to ensure that waiting brothers and sisters also receive their opportunity.

Frequently Asked Questions on Rawdah Booking

What should I do if Nusuk displays "No Slots Available"?

Do not panic. Slots are dynamically released in batches. Refresh the app daily at regular intervals: 12:00 AM midnight KSA time, post-Fajr, and on Friday afternoons. In addition, when other pilgrims cancel their bookings, those slots immediately reappear in green on the calendar.

Does NOOR-E-HARAM assist pilgrims with Nusuk registration?

Yes. Every pilgrim booked under our Mumbai, Bangalore, or Hubli packages receives one-on-one pre-departure guidance from our tour managers to install, configure, and secure their Rawdah permit before boarding.

Can I enter the Rawdah multiple times during a 15-day Umrah?

No. The Saudi Ministry's centralized electronic portal enforces a strict 365-day block once your passport number has been scanned. However, men can walk past the sacred chamber (Salam to the Prophet ﷺ) through the exterior barrier without a permit during non-prayer hours.
