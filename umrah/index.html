Frequently Asked Questions

How early should I book my Umrah tour?

We recommend booking at least 30 to 45 days in advance of your desired departure date. For peak seasons such as Ramadan or winter school holidays, early reservation 60 to 90 days in advance is strongly advised to secure direct flights and hotels closest to the Haram Sharif.

What documents are required for an Indian passport holder?

Indian citizens need an original passport valid for at least 6 months from the date of travel, 2 recent passport-size photographs with a white background, and a copy of their Aadhaar / PAN card. Our visa team coordinates the complete submission on the Saudi Ministry portal.

Do you provide assistance for senior citizens and wheelchair users?

Yes. We provide comprehensive support for elderly pilgrims including wheelchair assistance arrangements at airports and during Tawaf/Sa'i, ground-floor or elevator-adjacent room allocations, and special attention during Ziyarat excursions.
