The 4 Pillars of NOOR-E-HARAM Transparency

Pillar 1

No Hidden Surcharges

Every quoted package price includes the official Saudi electronic visa, mandatory CHI medical insurance, round-trip flights, courtyard lodging, 3 daily Indian buffet meals, and AC ground coach transit. You will never be asked for surprise airport fees.

Pillar 2

Real Distance Disclosures

We declare exact walking distances in meters (e.g. 150m from courtyard) rather than deceptive marketing claims. We specify whether hotels involve flat plaza access or steep hills, protecting elderly pilgrims from exhaustion.

Pillar 3

Scholar-Led Guidance

Every major group cohort is accompanied by certified Islamic scholars who conduct pre-departure workshops in Hubli, Bangalore, and Mumbai, guiding pilgrims through the 4 pillars of Umrah strictly according to the Sunnah.

Pillar 4

Legitimate Bank Accounts

All payments are accepted exclusively through official registered current accounts with proper computerized receipts and GST compliance. We strictly warn pilgrims never to transfer funds to personal savings accounts.

🛡️

Pilgrim Payment Protection Advisory

To safeguard against online pilgrimage fraud, ensure that any advance or balance payment is made directly to the official bank details provided on a stamped NOOR-E-HARAM invoice. Always demand a computerized booking confirmation receipt displaying your passport numbers and allocated travel dates.

Clear Cancellation & Refund Protocol

We recognize that unexpected illness, family emergencies, or visa delays can occur. Our cancellation terms are structured with complete fairness:

  • Cancellations 30+ Days Prior to Departure: 100% refund of advance payments minus nominal document verification handling fees and actual expenses already committed (such as non-refundable Saudi Ministry e-visa fees).
  • Cancellations 15 to 29 Days Prior: Refund calculated after deducting airline group ticket cancellation charges and non-refundable hotel deposit penalties.
  • Cancellations within 14 Days of Travel: Subject to strict non-refundable terms imposed by international scheduled airlines and Makkah/Madinah central hotels during peak pilgrimage seasons.
  • Emergency Pilgrim Replacement: In eligible packages, if a family member falls ill, we permit transfer of the seat to another immediate family member, subject to airline name change and visa re-issuance guidelines.

24/7 Field Support in Makkah & Madinah

When you arrive at King Abdulaziz International Airport (Jeddah) or Prince Mohammad bin Abdulaziz Airport (Madinah), our licensed ground team meets you inside the terminal to coordinate:

  • Baggage Loading: Direct transfer of luggage to your hotel rooms.
  • Step-Free Check-In: Key cards pre-allocated so weary pilgrims do not wait in crowded hotel lobbies.
  • Medical Emergency Escort: 24-hour liaison with Saudi Red Crescent hospital clinics inside the Haramain complex.
  • Lost Pilgrim Protocols: Every traveler is issued an identification wristband and hotel address card in Arabic and English with direct emergency phone numbers.

Frequently Asked Questions on Pilgrim Security

Can I visit your office in person before making a booking?

Absolutely. We strongly encourage prospective pilgrims to visit our Head Office Behind Barnabas Hall, Mantoor Road in Hubli, or meet our authorized local representatives across Dharwad, Haveri, Hangal, Savnur, Harihar, and Hospet to review contracts and itineraries in person.

Does NOOR-E-HARAM fabricate or purchase online reviews?

Never. In strict compliance with Islamic ethics and Google's merchant policies, we never buy fake reviews or invent artificial ratings. Every pilgrim testimonial is authentic and grounded in actual journeys served over our ten-year history.

What health insurance is provided on the Umrah visa?

Every electronic Umrah visa issued through our agency includes mandatory comprehensive coverage from the Council of Cooperative Health Insurance (CHI) of Saudi Arabia, covering emergency hospital admissions, ICU treatment, COVID-19 care, and medical repatriation up to SAR 100,000.
