Payment Policy

Official payment schedules, company bank account verification, and fraud prevention

Last Reviewed: 24 November 2026

⚠️ Critical Security Warning — Avoid Scams:

NOOR-E-HARAM never requests payments into personal savings accounts, unauthorized UPI IDs, or via unverified WhatsApp numbers. Always remit payments exclusively to our verified official company bank account. Demand an official, numbered stamped receipt for every transaction.

1. Payment Schedule & Milestones

To secure guaranteed airline seats, visa quotas, and hotel allotments, payments must follow our two-stage structure:

  • Stage 1 (At Booking): 50% advance payment along with original passports and passport photographs to initiate visa processing and lock air ticket PNRs.
  • Stage 2 (Final Settlement): Remaining 50% balance must be cleared 7 to 10 days prior to departure. Travel vouchers, flight tickets, and visa prints are issued upon complete balance reconciliation.

2. Approved Payment Modes

We accept payments through documented, secure banking channels only:

  • NEFT / RTGS / IMPS Direct Bank Transfer: Direct remittance to our designated corporate current account.
  • Official Company UPI: Scannable directly at our Hubli head office or on our official invoices.
  • Account Payee Cheque / Demand Draft: Drawn in favor of our official entity name: NOOR-E-HARAM Tours & Travels.
