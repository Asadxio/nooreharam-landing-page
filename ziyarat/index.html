Ziyarat Etiquette (Adab) for Pilgrims

  • Maintain Purity (Wudu): It is highly recommended to be in a state of Wudu, especially when visiting Masjid Quba where praying two Rakahs carries the reward of an Umrah.
  • Respecting Sunnah Rulings: Follow authentic Hadith guidelines regarding Duas and sending Salawat upon the Prophet ﷺ. Avoid unverified cultural innovations.
  • Protecting the Environment: Preserve the sacred cleanliness of the holy precincts and mountains by avoiding littering.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is climbing Jabal al-Nour (Ghar Hira) included in the bus tour?

Our tour bus stops at the base of Jabal al-Nour where scholars explain the descent of the first revelation (Surah Al-Alaq). Due to the steep 2-hour physical climb, ascending to the cave itself is optional and undertaken individually during early dawn hours when temperatures are cooler.

What are the virtues of visiting Masjid Quba on Saturdays?

As documented in Sahih Bukhari, the Prophet ﷺ used to visit Masjid Quba every Saturday, walking or riding, and would offer two units of prayer. We coordinate our Madinah Ziyarat schedule to honor this Sunnah whenever possible.
