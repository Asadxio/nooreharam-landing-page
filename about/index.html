About NOOR-E-HARAM

Founded on the uncompromising pillars of religious authenticity, operational honesty, and sincere Khidmat (service) to the guests of Allah (Dhuyuf ar-Rahman).

Our Founding Story

In 2016, NOOR-E-HARAM was established in Hubli, Karnataka, by Dr. Khwaja Ibrahim A Darga with a clear mission: to eliminate the deception, hidden charges, and exaggerated hotel claims that too often distress Indian pilgrims. Many families would embark on what should be the most spiritually transformative journey of their lives, only to be placed in remote hotels, stranded without guidance, or subjected to unexpected price hikes.

We built NOOR-E-HARAM on the principle that serving pilgrims is not a transactional business, but a sacred religious trust (Amanah). Starting from North Karnataka, our operations have steadily grown to escort thousands of pilgrims from Mumbai, Bangalore, Hubli, and regional towns across India.

Our Core Operating Values

1. Truth in Distance

We never exaggerate walking distances. Every meter is measured to the courtyard entrance, ensuring elderly pilgrims are never misled.

2. Scholar Accompaniment

Every group is guided by certified Islamic scholars fluent in Urdu, English, and Kannada to conduct rituals strictly per Sunnah.

3. Fixed Price Guarantee

Once confirmed in writing, your quote is fixed. We do not demand unexpected surcharges at the airport or hotel desk.

Leadership & Scholar Guidance

Our tours are led under the direct supervision of Dr. Khwaja Ibrahim A Darga alongside senior scholars. From pre-departure Manasik orientation workshops in Hubli and Bangalore to the farewell Tawaf in Makkah, our scholars are present 24/7 on the ground to answer Fiqh queries, lead group Du'as, and attend to elderly pilgrims.
