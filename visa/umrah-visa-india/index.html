Saudi Umrah Visa for Indian Citizens

The complete 2026–2027 guide to required documents, photo specifications, Mahram regulations, Nusuk app registration, and processing timelines.

Securing your Saudi Umrah electronic visa is straightforward when your paperwork strictly complies with Ministry guidelines. In this guide, NOOR-E-HARAM outlines the exact checklist and steps required for Indian passport holders to guarantee error-free visa issuance.

1. Detailed Document Checklist

  • Valid Indian Passport: Original passport or high-resolution color scan of the first and last bio-pages. The passport must remain valid for a minimum of 6 months from the date of departure.
  • Photographs: 2 recent color passport-size photos (35mm x 45mm or 2x2 inches) with an entirely white background, 80% face coverage, no glare on eyeglasses, and clear facial visibility.
  • PAN Card Copy: Required for Reserve Bank of India (RBI) LRS / travel remittance documentation.
  • Meningitis Vaccination Certificate: Quadrivalent ACWY vaccination certificate issued by an authorized center, stamped and signed by a registered medical practitioner.
  • Proof of Relationship (When Applicable): Marriage certificate or birth certificate if traveling with female dependents requiring Mahram documentation.

2. Female Pilgrims & Mahram Regulations

Official Saudi Ministry Rules on Women Traveling Without Mahram

Under official regulations from the Saudi Ministry of Hajj and Umrah, women of all ages are legally permitted to obtain an Umrah visa and travel to Saudi Arabia without a male Mahram. 3. The Nusuk App & Rawdah Sharif Permit

Every pilgrim visiting Madinah Munawwarah must obtain an official appointment to pray in the blessed Rawdah ash-Sharifah (the Garden of Paradise) inside Al-Masjid an-Nabawi. This is conducted through the official Saudi government app called Nusuk (formerly Eatmarna):

  • Once your Umrah visa is issued, download the Nusuk application on your smartphone.
  • Register using your Visa Number and Passport Number.
  • Select your preferred date and time slot for the Rawdah visit for Men or Women.
  • Our tour group leaders assist every pilgrim in securing their Nusuk slot so no one misses this blessed opportunity.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I perform Umrah on a Saudi tourist e-visa?

Yes. If you hold a valid, previously used US, UK, or Schengen tourist/business visa, you may qualify for the Saudi tourist e-visa, which also permits performing Umrah outside the official Hajj season.

Is biometric submission required for Indian pilgrims?

Indian Umrah travelers can complete their biometric self-enrollment (facial and fingerprint capture) digitally via the official Saudi Visa Bio mobile app before final visa issuance.
