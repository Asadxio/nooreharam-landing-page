Umrah Rules for Women 2026–2027

The authoritative, scholar-grounded guide for female pilgrims. Understand official Saudi Ministry visa regulations regarding Mahram accompaniment, traditional Fiqh rulings, Ihram clothing codes, menstruation rules, and dedicated Rawdah visiting windows.

Navigating Legal Regulations vs. Religious Fiqh

For decades, female pilgrims from India encountered confusion regarding whether they could undertake Umrah without an immediate male guardian (Mahram). To understand your rights and responsibilities, it is essential to clearly distinguish between Saudi governmental civil regulations and classical Islamic jurisprudence (Fiqh).

1. Official Saudi Civil Law: Mahram Requirement Removed

The Saudi Ministry of Hajj and Umrah officially announced that women of all ages are legally permitted to travel to Saudi Arabia for Hajj or Umrah without a Mahram. Under this legal framework:

  • No Minimum Age Cutoff: The historical rule restricting solo travel to women aged 45 and above with a notarized No Objection Certificate (NOC) is no longer enforced by Saudi immigration authorities.
  • Direct Electronic Visas: Women can apply for and receive electronic Umrah visas or Saudi tourist visas in their own name without linking a male passport number.
  • Border & Airport Entry: Saudi passport control officers at Jeddah (JED) and Madinah (MED) admit female travelers arriving independently, with female companions, or within registered commercial groups.

Government of India Haj Policy 2027: Ladies Without Mehram (LWM) Rules

Under Clause 6 of the official Haj Policy 2027 issued by the Haj Committee of India (Ministry of Minority Affairs, GoI):

  • Women Aged 45 & Above: Officially eligible to perform pilgrimage without a male Mehram if their school of thought (Maslak) permits, traveling in groups of 4 or more, or individually with HCoI/private cohort coordination. The Consulate General of India in Jeddah provides separate women-only accommodations.
  • LWM Aged 65 & Above (Mandatory Female Companion): In view of the strenuous physical demands, any LWM pilgrim aged 65 or above must be accompanied by a female companion aged 45 to 60 years. Lone registration is prohibited for elderly safety.

2. The Fiqh Perspective Across the Four Madhhabs

While Saudi law provides civil entry permission, pilgrims also seek alignment with their personal religious conscience. Islamic scholars across the major schools of jurisprudence offer distinct rulings:

School of Thought Core Ruling on Traveling for Umrah Without Mahram
Shafi'i & Maliki Madhhab Permits a woman to travel for her mandatory pilgrimage without a Mahram if she travels in safe, trustworthy female company (Al-Rafiqah Al-Thiqah) where moral and physical protection is assured.
Hanafi Madhhab Traditionally maintains that a woman should be accompanied by a husband or unmarriageable male relative (Mahram) for any journey exceeding three days of travel (approx. 77 km / 48 miles).
Hanbali Madhhab Traditionally requires a Mahram as a prerequisite for the obligation of Hajj, though modern contemporary scholars permit reliable group transit.

3. Ihram Dress Code & Prohibitions for Women

Unlike men, who are restricted to two unstitched white towels, women do not have a prescribed uniform. However, specific religious prohibitions apply while in Ihram:

Permissible Attire

  • Ordinary Modest Clothes: Any loose, non-transparent Abaya and Hijab in black, white, brown, or neutral tones.
  • Regular Footwear: Cushioned walking sneakers, leather shoes, or sandals with socks are completely permissible.
  • Inner Layering: Leggings, inner trousers, and secure sports bras to prevent friction during Tawaf and Sa'i.

Strict Prohibitions in Ihram

  • No Tied Niqab: Tying a stitched face-veil (Niqab) is prohibited (Sahih Bukhari). If passing men, a woman may draw her headscarf loosely over her face without tying.
  • No Gloves: Wearing stitched gloves on the hands is forbidden while in Ihram.
  • No Scented Cosmetics: Scented soap, lotions, kajal, or perfumes are strictly barred.

4. Menstruation (Hayd) Rules During Umrah

Many female pilgrims experience deep anxiety regarding natural biological cycles coinciding with their pilgrimage. Islamic jurisprudence provides compassionate clarity:

  • Entering Ihram at the Miqat: Even if menstruation has begun, a woman must still enter the state of Ihram at the Miqat (taking a bath for cleanliness, making Niyyah, and reciting the Talbiyah).
  • Prohibition of Tawaf: The Prophet (ﷺ) instructed Aisha (RA): "Do everything that a pilgrim does, except do not circumambulate the House until you are purified" (Sahih al-Bukhari). A woman in Hayd cannot enter the mosque prayer areas or perform Tawaf.
  • Sa'i & Ziyarat: Sa'i between Safa and Marwa cannot precede Tawaf. However, she may visit historical Ziyarat landmarks (Uhud, Quba courtyard, Badr), engage in continuous Dhikr, recite Duas from memory, and seek blessings.
  • Medical Postponement: Under clinical supervision, many women choose to take physician-prescribed hormonal tablets to safely delay their cycle for the duration of the 15-day pilgrimage.

5. Dedicated Rawdah Visiting Windows for Women

Masjid an-Nabawi provides segregated, security-monitored entrance corridors for female pilgrims visiting Riyadh ul-Jannah. In accordance with the Nusuk App System, women assemble at the Northern Courtyard:

  • Morning Window: Post-Fajr (approx. 6:00 AM) to 11:00 AM daily.
  • Night Window: Post-Isha (approx. 9:30 PM) to 2:00 AM daily.
  • Designated Assembly Gates: Gate 24, Gate 29, or Gate 32 (Northern courtyards).
  • Permit Check: Female security guards scan the digital QR permit from your smartphone screen.

Frequently Asked Questions on Women's Umrah

How does a woman exit Ihram (Tahallul)?

Unlike men who shave their entire head (Halq), women only perform Taqsir. A woman cuts approximately the length of a fingertip (about one inch or 2.5 cm) from the ends of her gathered hair in privacy or with a female companion/Mahram.

Can female friends share a room together on NOOR-E-HARAM packages?

Yes. We organize dedicated all-female quad, triple, or twin sharing rooms in verified courtyard hotels near the Haram, allowing sisters, mothers, and daughters to room comfortably together.

Is it safe for a woman to perform Tawaf alone at night?

Masjid al-Haram and Masjid an-Nabawi are among the safest places on earth, protected by thousands of security personnel, 24/7 CCTV surveillance, and specialized female security corps. However, for spiritual comfort, our tour groups walk to prayers together.
