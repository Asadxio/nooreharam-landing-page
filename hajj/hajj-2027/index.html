Government of India Haj Policy 2027: Quota & Regulatory Framework

In accordance with the official Haj Policy 2027 issued by the Haj Committee of India (HCoI), Ministry of Minority Affairs (GoI), India's bilateral Hajj quota with the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia is divided into two primary avenues:

Authority / Channel Quota Allocation Selection Mechanism Key Operational Distinctions
Haj Committee of India (HCoI) 70% of National Quota State-wise Computerized Qurrah (Randomized Draw of Lots) Standard government-organized group pilgrimage, Standard Azizia accommodation, fixed charter schedules.
Haj Group Organisers (HGOs) 30% of National Quota Direct Enrollment with Authorized Private Organizers Assured seat allocation without lottery uncertainty, flexible room sharing (couples & families), dedicated scholar escort, tailored transit.

Official Pilgrim Eligibility, Cover Size & Age Regulations

Clause 12

Senior Citizens (65+ Category)

Due to the arduous physical demands of Hajj (walking 15–25 km daily in extreme climatic conditions), pilgrims aged 65 or above must be accompanied by an eligible companion aged 18 to 65. Lone registrations are barred under policy.

Clause 6

Ladies Without Mehram (LWM)

Women aged 45 and above whose Maslak permits are officially eligible to travel for Hajj without a male Mehram. For LWM pilgrims aged 65 or above, a female companion aged 45 to 60 is mandatory.

Clause 2 & 7

Cover Size & Repeaters

HCoI enforces a 'Once in a Lifetime' rule with exceptions only for repeaters acting as Mehrams or companions for 65+ pilgrims. Covers allow a minimum of 1 and maximum of 5 immediate family members.

Two-Step Medical Screening & Mandatory Vaccination Protocol

Official Health Clearance Process (Haj Policy Clauses 21–24)

Haj Policy 2027 enforces a mandatory two-step medical clearance process: (1) An initial physical evaluation and fitness certificate issued by a registered Government Allopathic Doctor at the application stage, and (2) Pre-departure health re-examination at the embarkation point. Selected pilgrims receive an official Health & Training (HAT) Card.

  • Meningitis Vaccine: Quadrivalent Meningococcal Meningitis (ACYW-135) administered within the last 3 years and at least 10 days before flight departure.
  • Oral Polio Vaccine (OPV): Mandatory dose administered at the embarkation camp regardless of previous vaccination history.
  • Seasonal Influenza: Recommended quadrivalent flu vaccination administered at least 2 weeks prior to departure.
  • Medical Disqualifications: End-stage organ failure (cardiac, respiratory, renal, hepatic), active cancer undergoing chemo/radiotherapy, advanced dementia, and active contagious infections are disqualified from travel.

Frequently Asked Questions — Hajj 2027

How are rooms allocated in Azizia during Hajj?

In Azizia, accommodations are arranged in comfortable residential buildings separated by gender (typically 4 to 5 pilgrims per room). Private rooms for couples or family groups are available at an additional premium upon early request.

How does the scholar guide pilgrims during the Jamarat stoning?

Our Islamic scholar and group leads coordinate departures to the Jamarat during lower-temperature, low-congestion time slots approved by Saudi civil defense authorities, guiding the group closely and offering rulings for elderly pilgrims who qualify for proxy stoning (Niyabah).

How does Government of India Haj Policy 2027 divide quota between HCoI and private HGOs?

Under the official Haj Policy 2027 issued by the Ministry of Minority Affairs (GoI), 70% of India's bilateral Hajj quota is allocated to the Haj Committee of India (HCoI) distributed via state-wise randomized Qurrah (lottery), while 30% is allocated to registered Private Haj Group Organizers (HGOs). NOOR-E-HARAM operates within this authorized private framework to provide assured departures, custom room options, and direct scholar accompaniment.

What are the companion rules for pilgrims aged 65 and above under Haj Policy 2027?

Due to the strenuous nature of the pilgrimage (walking up to 25 km daily in extreme heat), Haj Policy 2027 mandates that pilgrims aged 65 or above must be accompanied by an eligible companion aged 18 to 65 years. For Ladies Without Mehram (LWM) aged 65 or above, the companion must be a female aged between 45 and 60 years.

Can women travel for Hajj without a Mehram under Haj Policy 2027?

Yes. Women aged 45 and above whose school of thought (Maslak) permits are officially eligible to perform Hajj without a male Mehram under the Ladies Without Mehram (LWM) category. The Consulate General of India in Jeddah arranges dedicated women-only accommodations in Makkah and Madinah.
