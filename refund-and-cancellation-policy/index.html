2. Non-Refundable Statutory & Supplier Components

Irrespective of cancellation timing, the following direct costs incurred with third parties cannot be recovered once issued:

  • Saudi Ministry Visa Fees & Mandatory Health Insurance: Once issued on the official Saudi portal, electronic visa fees are non-refundable per Saudi government regulations.
  • Non-Refundable Airline Group PNRs: Group booking flight tickets are governed by airline charter cancellation rules.
  • Bank & Payment Gateway Charges: Transaction fees, GST, and foreign exchange conversion charges.

3. Refund Processing Timeline

Approved refunds are processed via NEFT/RTGS bank transfer to the pilgrim's verified bank account within the management-confirmed timeline (⏳ To be confirmed by management) following formal audit of unutilized supplier funds. Cash refunds are strictly prohibited under Indian tax and banking laws.

4. How to Submit a Cancellation Request

To request a cancellation, please email our office at info@nooreharam.com with the subject line: Cancellation Request - [Booking ID / PNR] - [Pilgrim Name], attaching the original booking invoice and cancelled cheque / bank account details.
