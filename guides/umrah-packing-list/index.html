Umrah Packing List 2026–2027

The definitive, battle-tested packing checklist for Indian pilgrims. Master the religious requirements of Ihram clothing, unscented hygiene, marble-safe footwear, essential health supplies, and airline baggage limits.

Why Thoughtful Packing Protects Your Ibadah

Performing Umrah involves walking between 7 to 12 kilometers during the initial rituals of Tawaf and Sa'i alone, navigating high-density crowds on hard marble floors, and transitioning between air-conditioned halls and dry Saudi desert heat. Preparing the right gear beforehand eliminates unnecessary distractions, physical agony, and unintentional violations of Ihram regulations.

Below is the structured checklist developed through decades of guiding pilgrims departing from our Mumbai, Bangalore, and Hubli tour groups.

1. Ihram Wear & Modest Clothing

For Men

  • 2 Sets of Ihram Towels: Thick, 100% absorbent cotton unstitched white sheets (one for upper body / Rida, one for lower body / Izar). Bringing two sets ensures you have a clean spare if one gets stained.
  • Ihram Belt / Money Pouch: Stitched belts with secure zip compartments for passports and cash are fully permissible in Ihram.
  • Safety Pins: Large safety pins to hold the Izar firmly at the waist.
  • Regular Modest Wear: 3–4 sets of comfortable Kurta Pajama or Jubba for non-Ihram days in Makkah and Madinah.

For Women

  • 3–4 Abayas: Lightweight, breathable, opaque cotton/linen abayas in black or muted earth tones suitable for Saudi warmth.
  • Hijabs & Under-caps: 4–5 soft cotton hijabs and snug under-caps to prevent hair slippage during prayer and prostration.
  • Prayer Dresses: Loose cotton garments for easy resting in hotel rooms between prayer times.
  • Cotton Inner Wear: Breathable leggings or shalwars to wear beneath abayas to prevent chafing during long walks.

2. Strictly Fragrance-Free Toiletries

Crucial Rule: Applying scents to the body or clothing while in Ihram is prohibited. Standard soap, scented shampoos, or perfume will incur religious penalties. Pack these items beforehand as finding genuinely 100% unscented supplies around the Haram during peak season can be difficult:

  • Unscented Soap / Body Wash: Pure glycerin or unscented Castilian soap bars (store in a sealed plastic soap box).
  • Fragrance-Free Shampoo: Hypoallergenic, zero-perfume travel bottles.
  • Unscented Petroleum Jelly / Vaseline: Invaluable for men to apply to the inner thighs prior to entering Ihram to prevent painful skin chafing during Tawaf and Sa'i.
  • Unscented Wet Wipes: Alcohol-free, 100% fragrance-free wipes for quick hand cleaning.
  • Dental Care: Miswak or unscented toothpaste and soft toothbrush.
  • Small Scissors & Disposable Razors: Kept in checked luggage only for Halq (shaving head) or Taqsir (trimming hair) at Tahallul.

3. Footwear for Marble Floors

Category Recommended Specifications Important Rule
Men (During Ihram) Open-toe leather or rubber sandals with arch support. Must leave ankles and top bridge of foot completely exposed.
Men (Non-Ihram) Cushioned walking sneakers or slip-on loafers. Permissible anytime outside the state of Ihram.
Women (All Times) Comfortable walking shoes, orthopedic sneakers, or anti-slip flats. No footwear restrictions in Ihram, but comfort is paramount.
Shoe Bag (All) Lightweight drawstring shoe pouch with shoulder cords. Keep your shoes with you inside the Haram rather than losing them in public shoe racks.

4. Medicines & Personal Health Kit

Keep your primary medication in your cabin bag alongside a signed English prescription from your physician. We recommend packing a compact first-aid kit containing:

  • Prescription Drugs: Enough supply for the entire 15 or 21-day itinerary plus 5 days extra buffer.
  • Pain & Fever Relief: Paracetamol (Crocin/Calpol) or Ibuprofen for muscle aches after the 7 circuits of Tawaf and Sa'i.
  • Throat Lozenges & Cough Syrup: Essential for the famous "Umrah cough" caused by cold air conditioning clashing with outdoor desert heat.
  • Hydration & Electrolytes: 10–15 Oral Rehydration Salts (ORS) packets to mix with Zamzam water.
  • Digestive Care: Antacids (Digene/Eno) and anti-diarrheal tablets (Loperamide/Imodium).
  • Blister Bandages & Athletic Tape: Padded adhesive plasters for friction blisters on feet.

5. Electronics, Adapters & Travel Logistics

  • Saudi Power Adapters: Saudi Arabia uses Type G 3-pin plugs (British standard, 220V, 50Hz). Bring a universal adapter.
  • Power Bank: Maximum 10,000 mAh to 20,000 mAh (under 100 watt-hours). Must be packed in hand carry; strictly forbidden in checked baggage.
  • Neck Wallet / Document Holder: Worn under your clothing to safeguard your passport, Saudi e-visa printout, and hotel address card.
  • Collapsible Water Bottle: Lightweight silicone bottle to refill with free chilled Zamzam water from dispensers inside the Haram.

Frequently Asked Questions on Packing for Umrah

What are the airline baggage rules for Indian Umrah flights?

Standard economy flights (Air India, Saudia, IndiGo, Gulf carriers) typically offer 2 pieces of 23 kg each (total 46 kg) or 30 kg total check-in, plus 7 kg cabin baggage. In addition, an official 5-litre Zamzam canister is carried free of charge on return flights for Umrah visa holders.

Can I buy unscented soap and toiletries in Makkah?

Yes, pharmacies in the Clock Tower and around the Haram stock unscented soaps, but they are often sold at high tourist premiums or sell out during Ramadan. We strongly recommend purchasing your unscented toiletries in India before departure.

Do women need to pack specific colors for their Abayas?

No. While black is traditional and widespread in Saudi Arabia, women may wear white, brown, blue, or any modest, loose-fitting, non-transparent color that does not attract undue attention.
