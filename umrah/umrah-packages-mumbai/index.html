Mumbai Departure Details & Flight Logistics

Flight Routing & Baggage Guidelines

Departures originate from Terminal 2 of Mumbai Airport (BOM). Typical flight time to King Abdulaziz International Airport (JED) is approximately 4 hours and 45 minutes on direct carriers. Baggage allowance generally permits 2 checked bags (23kg each) plus 7kg hand luggage and 5 litres of sealed Zamzam water on the return leg, as permitted by airline guidelines.

Frequently Asked Questions — Mumbai Departures

Can pilgrims from outside Mumbai join the Mumbai departure group?

Yes. Many pilgrims from Pune, Nashik, Surat, and across Maharashtra and Gujarat join our Mumbai groups. Our team assists with airport reporting times and terminal coordination at BOM Terminal 2.

How is the Saudi Umrah visa processed for Mumbai travelers?

You simply need to share clear digital copies of your passport front and back pages, white-background photographs, and PAN card. Our team processes the official electronic visa with medical insurance included.
