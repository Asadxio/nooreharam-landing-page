Frequently Asked Questions

How can Indian citizens apply for an Umrah visa?

Our authorized team submits your visa application directly through the official Saudi Ministry of Hajj & Umrah portal. You only need to provide a 6-month valid passport, photograph, and PAN card. Processing typically completes within 3 to 7 working days.

Can women travel for Umrah without a Mahram?

Under current Saudi regulations, women aged 45 and above can travel for Umrah without a physical Mahram when traveling as part of an organized, licensed group like NOOR-E-HARAM.
