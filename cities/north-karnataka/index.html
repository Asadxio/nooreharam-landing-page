North Karnataka Umrah & Hajj Hub

Comprehensive pilgrimage coordination tailored for families across Hubli-Dharwad, Haveri, Hangal, Savnur, Harihar, Hospet, and Belgaum. Doorstep document handling, multilingual scholar orientation in Kannada and Urdu, and dedicated feeder transport to Mumbai and Bangalore departure gates.

Deep Regional Roots: Serving North Karnataka for Over a Decade

Headquartered Behind Barnabas Hall on Mantoor Road in Hubli, NOOR-E-HARAM Tours & Travels has established itself as the trusted pilgrimage partner for thousands of Muslim families across the commercial and agricultural heartland of North Karnataka. While metropolitan agencies treat regional pilgrims as an afterthought, our entire infrastructure is built to provide seamless, door-to-door care from your hometown to the Holy Kaaba.

The Integrated Feeder Transit Network

The greatest hurdle for regional pilgrims is traveling to distant international departure airports with heavy luggage and elderly family members. We solve this through our synchronized feeder transit network:

Transit Route 1

Hubli to Mumbai (BOM) Direct

Direct luxury AC multi-axle sleeper coaches depart our Hubli Head Office, picking up travelers at designated halts in Dharwad and Belgaum, arriving directly at Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport (Terminal 2) departure gates.

Transit Route 2

Hubli to Bangalore (BLR) Direct

Dedicated group transfers connecting Haveri, Harihar, and Davanagere directly to Kempegowda International Airport (Terminal 2) for direct Saudi Airlines and IndiGo flights to Jeddah and Madinah.

Frequently Asked Questions for Regional Travelers

Can I make cash payments at the local branch desks?

All payments must be processed with an official computerized receipt. In accordance with RBI and banking norms, bank transfers (NEFT/RTGS/IMPS) or cheques drawn to our official NOOR-E-HARAM current account are strongly preferred for security and instant verification.

What luggage allowances apply on group feeder buses?

Our luxury feeder coaches feature spacious undercarriage luggage compartments that accommodate your full airline check-in allowance (typically two 23 kg suitcases or one 30 kg bag plus hand luggage) without excess fees.

Do you assist with passport renewal for North Karnataka pilgrims?

Yes. Our Hubli Head Office team guides prospective pilgrims with online appointment booking at the Regional Passport Office (RPO) in Hubli or local Post Office Passport Seva Kendras (POPSK).
