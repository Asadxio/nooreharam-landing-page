General Pilgrim Contract & Service Guidelines

Last Reviewed: 24 November 2026

Important Notice: By booking any Hajj, Umrah, or Ziyarat tour package with NOOR-E-HARAM, the pilgrim ("traveler") acknowledges and accepts these binding Terms and Conditions. Please review them carefully before remitting any advance payment.

1. Scope of Service

NOOR-E-HARAM operates as an authorized Islamic pilgrimage tour organizer, facilitator, and coordinator. Our services include processing Saudi Umrah visas, reserving airline seats on scheduled flights, arranging hotel bookings in the holy cities of Makkah al-Mukarramah and Madinah al-Munawwarah, coordinating air-conditioned coach transfers, providing daily catering, and offering religious guidance through certified scholars.

2. Booking Confirmation & Fixed Price Guarantee

  • Display package rates on our website are starting prices based on standard room sharing tiers (Quad/Triple/Double).
  • Fixed Price Guarantee: Once a pilgrim has submitted required documents and paid the designated booking deposit, the package cost is confirmed in writing on an official stamped invoice. This price is locked and will not be altered unless the pilgrim requests post-booking itinerary modifications.
  • Seat confirmation is subject to passport validity (minimum 6 months from travel date) and Saudi visa approval.

3. Visa Approval & Saudi Ministry Regulations

Visa issuance is governed solely by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs (KSA) and the Saudi Ministry of Hajj & Umrah. While NOOR-E-HARAM maintains high submission success through verified channels, visa issuance remains the sovereign prerogative of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. We are not liable for delays, rejections, or policy amendments enacted by the Saudi Government.

4. Flight Schedules & Airline Policies

  • Group flight tickets (Saudia, IndiGo, Air India, etc.) are issued under non-refundable group blocking contracts.
  • Airlines reserve the right to reschedule departure times, terminals, or flight numbers due to air traffic control or operational reasons. NOOR-E-HARAM will coordinate and assist pilgrims in all instances of airline schedule revisions.
  • Baggage limits (typically 20–40KG check-in + 7KG cabin + 5L Zamzam) must be strictly adhered to by the pilgrim. Airline excess baggage charges are the sole responsibility of the passenger.

5. Hotel Accommodation & Distances

Hotel check-in and check-out timings in Makkah and Madinah adhere to Saudi hospitality standards (typically Check-in: 4:00 PM; Check-out: 12:00 PM). Stated distances to the courtyard (e.g., 50m, 250m, 750m) represent verified pedestrian walking routes from the hotel lobby to the outer marble courtyard of the Haramain.

6. Pilgrim Conduct & Adherence to Local Law

Pilgrims are expected to maintain Islamic decorum, observe group discipline, and abide by the laws and customs of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. The company cannot be held responsible for any legal penalties, fines, or confiscations arising from individual violations of Saudi customs, security, or immigration rules.
