Bangalore Departure FAQs

Can senior citizens from Bangalore get wheelchair assistance at BLR airport?

Yes. When booking your package, notify our team of any mobility requirements. We pre-book airline wheelchair assistance from check-in at Kempegowda Airport (BLR) straight through arrival in Saudi Arabia.

How can I book from Bangalore if your head office is in Hubli?

Our booking process is completely digital and seamless. You can submit documents securely via WhatsApp or email, receive formal booking confirmations and receipts, and meet our airport representative directly at Kempegowda Airport on the day of departure.
