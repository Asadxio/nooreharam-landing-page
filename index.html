Pilgrim Preparation

Before Departure

Everything you need to prepare before beginning your sacred journey to the Haramain.

🧳 What to Pack

  • Passport & Visa Documents
  • Ihram Garments (for men)
  • Modest Islamic Clothing
  • Comfortable Walking Footwear
  • Essential Medicines & First Aid
  • Toiletries (unscented during Ihram)
  • Prayer Essentials — Tasbih, Quran
  • Universal Power Adapter
  • Mobile Charger & Power Bank
  • Light Jacket for AC Spaces

💰 Currency Guide

  • Saudi Riyal (SAR) is the local currency
  • Exchange at authorized money changers
  • Carry sufficient cash (SAR 500–1000 minimum)
  • Cards accepted in major hotels
  • ATMs widely available near Haramain
  • Avoid exchanging at airports (poor rates)
  • Inform your bank of travel dates

📱 SIM Card Guide

  • STC, Mobily, Zain are major Saudi providers
  • Buy prepaid SIM at airport arrivals
  • Data packages from SAR 15–50
  • Check international roaming with your provider
  • WhatsApp calls work on Saudi SIMs
  • Save Noor-E-Haram support: +91 9986925592

🏥 Health Advice

  • Stay well-hydrated at all times
  • Practice walking 2–3 weeks before travel
  • Use sunscreen and carry an umbrella
  • Carry medications with doctor's prescription
  • Elderly pilgrims: inform our team for special arrangements
  • Wear breathable, loose-fitting clothing
  • Rest adequately between Ziyarat visits

💉 Vaccination

  • Meningitis ACWY vaccine — mandatory for Saudi visa
  • COVID-19 vaccination per current Saudi guidelines
  • Influenza vaccine — strongly recommended
  • Carry vaccination certificates
  • Check Saudi Ministry of Health guidelines before travel
  • Consult your doctor 4–6 weeks before departure

📜 Saudi Travel Rules

  • Respect local laws and Islamic etiquette at all times
  • Modest dress required in all public spaces
  • Photography restricted near government buildings
  • No public display of affection
  • Follow airport security procedures calmly
  • Keep your documents secure at all times
