Pre-Departure Preparation Checklist

  • Physical Fitness: Performing Tawaf and Sa'i involves walking several kilometers on marble surfaces. We encourage pilgrims to practice daily walking 3 to 4 weeks prior to departure.
  • Ihram Essentials: For men, two seamless white sheets of cloth; for women, regular modest clothing conforming to Hijab requirements.
  • Personal Medications: Carry an adequate supply of prescribed medicines accompanied by a doctor's English prescription in original packaging.
  • Footwear: Comfortable slip-on shoes or sandals with good arch support and non-slip soles suitable for hot stone pavements.
