Compliance & Data Protection

Privacy Policy

Aligned with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 & Indian Information Technology Rules

Last Reviewed: 24 November 2026

Key Summary: NOOR-E-HARAM ("we", "our", "us") values the sanctity and privacy of every pilgrim. We collect personal data strictly to issue Saudi Umrah/Hajj visas, book airline tickets, reserve hotel accommodation in Makkah & Madinah, and coordinate ground transport. We never sell or monetize your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

When you inquire, register, or book an Umrah or Hajj pilgrimage through our website, office, or official communication channels, we may collect the following categories of information:

  • Identity & Contact Information: Full legal name (as per passport), phone number, email address, residential address, emergency contact details, and relation.
  • Travel & Visa Documentation: Passport copies (first and last page), national ID, passport-size photographs, Mahram relationship documents (where applicable under Saudi law), and polio/meningitis ACWY vaccination records.
  • Payment Information: Bank transaction reference numbers (UTR), payment mode, and receipt copies. (Note: We do not store credit card or debit card numbers on our servers; payments are processed directly through licensed banking channels).
  • Technical & Analytics Data: IP address, browser type, device information, and anonymous website engagement metrics collected via Google Analytics 4 (GA4).

2. Purpose & Legal Basis of Processing

Under the DPDP Act 2023, your personal data is collected and processed solely for specified, legitimate pilgrimage purposes:

  • To register and submit your electronic Umrah or Hajj visa applications via the Saudi Ministry of Hajj & Umrah / Nusuk portals.
  • To book confirmed PNR flight tickets with partner airlines (Saudia, IndiGo, Air India, etc.).
  • To register hotel room allocations in Makkah and Madinah compliant with municipal safety standards.
  • To coordinate group transfers, medical emergencies, and on-ground logistics.
  • To send itinerary confirmations, departure schedules, and pre-departure pilgrim orientation guidelines.

3. Data Sharing & Cross-Border Transfers

To fulfill your pilgrimage booking, your data is shared strictly on a need-to-know basis with authorized entities:

  • Saudi Regulatory Authorities: Ministry of Hajj & Umrah, Ministry of Foreign Affairs (KSA), and Nusuk portal.
  • Airlines & GDS Providers: For issuance of non-transferable international flight tickets.
  • Licensed Accommodation & Transport Providers: Hotels in Makkah/Madinah and authorized United Bus transport operators.

We do not share, lease, or sell your personal data to marketing agencies or third-party advertisers under any circumstances.

4. Data Retention & Security Measures

We retain your passport and booking data for the statutory period required under Indian travel regulations and tax audits, or until the pilgrimage tour is completed. Physical documentation is stored securely in locked archives, and digital records are protected by encryption, SSL/TLS, and restricted access controls.

5. Pilgrim Rights Under DPDP Act 2023

As a data principal, you have the following legal rights:

  • Right to Access: You may request a summary of your personal data processed by us.
  • Right to Correction & Erasure: You may request correction of inaccurate data or deletion of your records (subject to regulatory visa and accounting obligations).
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You may withdraw your consent for non-essential communications at any time.
