Authorized electronic visa processing for Indian citizens with verified document verification, mandatory health insurance, and transparent turnaround.

Obtaining a valid Saudi Umrah visa is the essential first legal step of your sacred journey. The Ministry of Hajj & Umrah processes all pilgrim visas electronically. At NOOR-E-HARAM, our dedicated visa documentation desk assists pilgrims from across India, ensuring accurate biometric and biographical data submission to prevent unnecessary delays.

Essential Document Checklist for Indian Citizens

  • Original Indian Passport: Valid for a minimum of 6 months beyond the intended travel date with at least 2 blank pages.
  • Photographs: 2 recent colored passport-size photographs taken against an all-white background with neutral facial expression.
  • PAN Card / Aadhaar Copy: Clear scan copies for verification and airline booking reconciliation.
  • Vaccination Record: Quadrivalent Meningitis vaccination certificate administered at an authorized health facility.
  • NOC (When Applicable): For female travelers below 45 years traveling without a direct Mahram, subject to current Saudi regulatory guidelines.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many days does Saudi Umrah visa processing take?

Under normal circumstances, once your documents are uploaded to the Saudi system by our team, approval is typically granted within 3 to 7 working days. During the busy month of Ramadan, we advise applying at least 3 weeks in advance.

Can I travel to other Saudi cities on an Umrah visa?

Yes. Current Saudi regulations allow Umrah visa holders to travel freely to all cities within the Kingdom, including Jeddah, Taif, and Riyadh, during their permitted duration of stay.
