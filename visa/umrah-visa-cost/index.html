Umrah E-Visa vs. 1-Year Tourist eVisa: Cost & Eligibility

Umrah E-Visa

Standard Umrah Visa

  • Eligibility: Open to all Indian passport holders through authorized agencies.
  • Validity: 90 days from date of issuance (Single Entry).
  • Permitted Activities: Umrah, Rawdah prayer, travel throughout all Saudi cities.
  • Zamzam Allowance: Official 5-litre Zamzam canister permitted on return flights.
  • Typical Cost: ₹14,000 to ₹17,500 all-inclusive.
Tourist eVisa

1-Year Multiple Entry eVisa

  • Eligibility: Indian passport holders who possess a valid, used US, UK, or Schengen visa, or GCC residency.
  • Validity: 1 year (Multiple entries, max 90 days per stay).
  • Permitted Activities: Tourism, leisure, and performing Umrah (except during Hajj season).
  • Zamzam Allowance: Airline policies vary; some budget carriers restrict Zamzam check-in.
  • Typical Cost: ~SAR 535 (~₹12,500) applied directly on visitsaudi portal.
⚠️

Beware of "₹5,000 Umrah Visa" Scams

Unscrupulous middlemen and unverified social media ads frequently advertise "Cheapest Umrah Visa for ₹5,000 to ₹7,000". These are either transit visas (only valid for 96 hours and ineligible for Zamzam carriage) or completely fraudulent counterfeit printouts. Entering the Kingdom on an unauthorized transit visa to perform full Umrah risks heavy fines, deportation, and entry bans.

What Documents Are Required to Process Your Visa?

  • Original Indian Passport Scan: Clear color scan of first and last page with minimum 6 months validity from date of travel.
  • Passport-Size Photograph: 2x2 inch white background photo (no glare, no head tilted, neutral facial expression).
  • Confirmed Travel Dates: Tentative arrival and departure dates to align with the electronic booking reference (BRN).
  • PAN Card: Required under Indian Reserve Bank (LRS) regulations for foreign remittance.

Frequently Asked Questions on Visa Pricing

Is the Umrah visa fee refundable if my travel plans change?

No. Once the Saudi Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MOFA) generates the electronic visa and policy number, all government fees and mandatory insurance premiums are non-refundable under Saudi law.

Are there extra visa fees for repeat pilgrims (Mukarrar)?

The historical SAR 2,000 repeat pilgrim fee was abolished by royal decree several years ago. Today, repeat pilgrims pay the exact same standard visa and insurance fee as first-time applicants.

How fast can NOOR-E-HARAM process my Umrah visa?

Standard electronic processing typically takes between 24 to 72 business hours after receiving your clear passport scans and verified details.
