Refund & Cancellation Policy

Clear deduction slabs, non-refundable components, and application process

Last Reviewed: 24 November 2026

Fair & Transparent Policy: We understand that unforeseen medical emergencies or family circumstances may force pilgrims to cancel their travel. Because group pilgrimage packages involve non-recoverable financial commitments (group air tickets, visa fees, hotel block reservations in Saudi Arabia), cancellations are subject to the standard deduction schedule below.

1. Cancellation Deduction Schedule (Slabs)

The refund amount is calculated based on the date written cancellation is received at our Hubli office or via official email (info@nooreharam.com) relative to the flight departure date:

Cancellation Notice Period Deduction / Retention Amount Estimated Refund Eligibility
45+ Days before departure Non-refundable Registration Fee (₹5,000 per person) + Visa costs (if issued) Balance advance refunded in full
30 to 44 Days before departure 25% of total package cost + Actual visa & airline issuance penalties Remaining balance refunded
15 to 29 Days before departure 50% of total package cost (Hotel room non-refundable block retention) Remaining balance refunded
08 to 14 Days before departure 75% of total package cost 25% of package cost refunded
0 to 07 Days or No-Show at Airport 100% of total package cost (Non-refundable) No refund available

Note: If the owner's operational contract mandates specific variance, the final deduction adheres to [[CANCELLATION_SLAB]].

2. Strictly Non-Refundable Components

Under all circumstances, the following fees incurred on behalf of the pilgrim cannot be refunded once processed:

  • Saudi Ministry Visa Fees & Mandatory Health Insurance: Once approved on the electronic portal, Saudi visa fees are 100% non-refundable by the Ministry.
  • Group PNR Flight Tickets: Group block airfares are issued under strict airline charter/group contracts and do not permit individual refunds.
  • Bank Transaction & Processing Fees: Statutory GST, merchant interchange fees, and international remittance bank charges.

3. Refund Processing Timeline

Approved refunds are processed via direct NEFT/RTGS bank transfer to the pilgrim's verified bank account within 14 to 21 working days following formal audit and receipt of cancellation approval. Cash refunds are strictly prohibited under anti-money laundering and income tax regulations.

4. How to Submit a Cancellation Request

To request a cancellation, please email our office at info@nooreharam.com with the subject line: Cancellation Request - [Booking ID / PNR] - [Pilgrim Name], attaching the original booking invoice and cancelled cheque / bank account details.
