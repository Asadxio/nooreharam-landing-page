The Five Sacred Days of Hajj — Our On-Ground Support

Day / Date Station Our Service & Guidance
8th Dhul Hijjah Mina (Day of Tarwiyah) Organized bus transfer from Azizia to assigned Maktab in Mina. Scholars lead Dhikr, prayers, and Talbiyah.
9th Dhul Hijjah Arafat & Muzdalifah Transit to Arafat for the pivotal Wuqoof. Khutbah and combined Zuhr/Asr prayers. Sunset departure to Muzdalifah for open-air stay and pebble collection.
10th Dhul Hijjah Mina & Makkah (Eid) Rami of Jamarat al-Aqaba, Qurbani (sacrifice) confirmation, Halq/Taqsir (exit from major Ihram), and group transport for Tawaf al-Ifadah.
11th–12th Dhul Hijjah Tashreeq Days (Mina) Stoning of all three Jamarat after Zawal under scholar coordination to avoid peak crowding. Return to Makkah.
